The Track

"Nokia" is a song by Canadian rapper Drake from his collaborative studio album with PartyNextDoor entitled "Some Sexy Songs 4 U". Written by Drake and produced by Paul Omar "Elkan" Agyei, it was released on February 14, 2025, by Santa Anna Label Group, OVO Sound and Republic Records.

Our Approach

  • Distributed 30,000 posts in just 1 week across TikTok and YouTube Shorts
  • Created viral moments with multiple hook variations
  • Targeted key markets in North America, UK, Australia and New Zealand
  • Coordinated massive push around the single release

The Results

"Nokia" peaked at number five on the Canadian Hot 100 and number two on the Billboard Hot 100 on April 19, 2025. Outside of North America, "Nokia" peaked within the top ten of the charts in Australia, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom. It impacted contemporary hit radio as the album's second single on March 11, 2025.

The Challenge

4rain, an emerging DJ and producer, released "Ella Ya Me Olvido" but struggled to break through in a saturated Latin music market. Despite the quality of the track, organic discovery was slow and streams remained low.

Our Approach

  • Created viral-focused clips highlighting the emotional hook
  • Distributed across Latin music communities and trending sound accounts
  • Tested multiple visual formats: lyrics, dance challenges, emotional storytelling
  • Coordinated push across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts

The Results

"Ella Ya Me Olvido" exploded on TikTok, becoming a viral sound in the Latin music community. The track reached 69 million Spotify streams, 1 million monthly listeners, and achieved triple platinum certification in Mexico. The success led to label deals and collaboration opportunities with major artists.
